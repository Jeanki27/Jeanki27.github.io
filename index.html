About Me

Security professional, engineer, and Marine

I am a cybersecurity professional and U.S. Marine Corps veteran based in Central Florida, and I hold an active Secret clearance. My path into security started in the Marines, where I served as an S1 Data Systems Administrator for a V-22 Osprey aviation squadron at Kaneohe Bay, Hawaii. There I set up and hardened user accounts in Department of Defense systems, handled password resets and access problems, and protected the personnel records and personal information of a large command. After the Marines I worked as an avionics maintenance technician, tracing aircraft faults through their system logs, and then as a lead desktop support engineer, where I patched and hardened Windows endpoints and set least privilege access in Active Directory and Group Policy.

Today I am a Platform Operations Engineer at Cavnue, a company that builds connected roadway infrastructure. I led the DevSecOps rollout that added container image scanning, secret detection, and software bills of materials to all 44 of our image repositories through one shared CI/CD template, and I replaced stored pipeline keys with keyless Google Cloud authentication and secret rotation. I also stood up a $1M colocation data center, take my turn on the incident response rotation, and work in the field on the roadside cameras and network hubs across Florida and Georgia.

I earned my Associate of Science in Information Technology from Full Sail University with High Honors and a 3.92 GPA, and I am finishing my Bachelor of Science in Cybersecurity at Full Sail this December with a 3.99 GPA. I hold CompTIA Security+ and ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity, and my goal is a security engineering role in defense or critical infrastructure. In this portfolio you will find a security program I built for a company called SnowBe Online, including the security plan, two policies, and a maturity assessment, along with a malware case study I worked through using the CIS Controls.
